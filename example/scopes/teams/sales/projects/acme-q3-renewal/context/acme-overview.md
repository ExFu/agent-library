# Acme Corp — overview

## Who they are

Mid-market manufacturing company, headquartered in Manchester. Roughly 1,200 employees. Family-owned for three generations; recent professionalisation of management under a new CFO.

## What they do

Industrial components and assemblies. Two main product lines: precision machining and contract assembly. Strong reputation for quality, less competitive on price.
